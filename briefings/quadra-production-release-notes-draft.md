## Recommended public notes (en-US)

Welcome to Quadra-M.I.N.D. Sight 1.0.0.

Train attention, awareness, and clear thinking with guided
four-quadrant cognitive exercises, metacognitive sessions, journaling,
and a binaural spatial audio lab.

What's in this first public release:
• Guided session runner with structured metacognitive exercises
• Four-quadrant (Quadra) training views and a personal dashboard
• Journal for reflections and session notes
• Audio lab with binaural and spatial soundscapes
• AI-guided facilitator and introspection tools (offline fallbacks included)
• Installable, private by design — your data stays on your device

We'd love your feedback as we keep improving the training experience.

## Why this replaces the staged notes

The staged production draft copied the Alpha closed-test text
("Welcome to the first closed test..."), which would confuse public users
and misdescribe the release. The draft above is audience-correct,
feature-accurate per the repo README (React PWA, session runner, journal,
audio lab, facilitator), and makes no medical, diagnostic, or guaranteed
outcome claims.
